What we check on every street

Every stretch of road gets a score from 0 to 100, recalculated for the hour you walk. After sunset, lighting and open shops count for more.

💡

Lighting

Street lamps and lit roads, plus broken lights people report. Matters most after dark.

Real sunset for your city
🏪

Open places

Shops, cafes, pharmacies and petrol pumps that are open at that moment. These are eyes on the street and places to step into.

Opening hours checked by the hour
👮

Police & CCTV

Distance to the nearest police station, and cameras along the way.

🌳

Isolated surroundings

Parks at night, construction sites, rail lines, scrubland and underpasses are flagged and avoided.

🚪

Ways out

Dead-end lanes and long stretches with no side streets score lower, because you can't turn off if something feels wrong.

🚌

Transport nearby

Bus stops, stations and taxi stands give you a quick exit.

📍

Reports & history

Anonymous reports from walkers ("felt unsafe", "harassment", "felt safe") and imported incident data, weighted by how recent they are and the time of day.

🌧️

Weather

Rain empties the streets, so scores drop a little when it's pouring.

Questions

Does a high score mean a route is safe?

No route is ever guaranteed safe. A score means "safer than the other options, based on what we know". We show the reasons and flag stretches with little data, so you can judge for yourself. Always trust your instincts.

Where does the data come from?

OpenStreetMap for roads, lamps, shops, opening hours, police and cameras. Anonymous reports from SafeRoute users. Incident data from partners and public sources where it's available, and live weather.

Which areas does it cover?

Anywhere OpenStreetMap has streets, which is almost everywhere. The first walk in a new neighbourhood takes a few extra seconds while we fetch and score its streets. Scores are most detailed where lamps, shops and opening hours are well mapped, and we tell you when data is thin.

Do I need to install anything?

No. It runs in your browser. Installing it to your home screen makes it open faster and work like an app, including the SOS shortcut.

Does SOS call the police automatically?

SOS texts your trusted contacts a live location link and puts a one-tap call to 112 in front of you. Phones don't let websites place calls on their own, so you stay in control.

How much does it cost?

SafeRoute is free for walkers. Campuses and organisations pay for custom mapping, data and branding.
